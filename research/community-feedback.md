# Community Feedback

## Scope

No community survey or user interview has been conducted for this project. This document therefore makes no claim that community feedback validates a feature, provider, or workflow. Community discussion, when collected later, will be treated as usability evidence rather than technical truth.

## Planned Research Protocol

| Question | Method | Decision Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is the chat-first interface understandable? | Moderated prototype tasks with first-time users. | Navigation and terminology refinement. |
| Do users understand risk labels and approval prompts? | Scenario-based usability testing. | Policy wording and confirmation UX. |
| Which integrations are valuable enough to justify security work? | Ranked survey and follow-up interviews. | Connector backlog prioritisation. |
| Are privacy explanations actionable? | Comprehension testing of just-in-time disclosures. | Privacy copy and controls. |

## Evidence Standard

Each future finding will record the participant count, recruitment criteria, task, raw observation, confidence, and product decision. Anonymous quotes will not substitute for policy, security, or API evidence.
