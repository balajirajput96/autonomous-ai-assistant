# Reel 0023 — Visual Provenance Plan

## Purpose

Explain a computational reward-prediction-error model as an expectation-versus-outcome mismatch without showing it as a literal scan of happiness, a diagnostic, or proof of subjective experience.

## Intended visual structure

The visual sequence uses three text-free scenes. Scene one contrasts a modest expected quiz token with a better-than-expected token; scene two uses an abstract balance/mismatch arc to show better-than-expected, expected and worse-than-expected outcomes; scene three shows an expectation marker being gently revised rather than a “dopamine rush.” Hindi captions remain the only explanatory text in the final MP4.

## Provenance rule

The preferred route is one original, text-free 9:16 master image if fresh image generation is available. If that route is unavailable, pending, or unsuitable, the final package may use original deterministic 2.5D illustrations generated locally with Pillow and animated/assembled with FFmpeg. That fallback must be declared in metadata and must never be called fresh AI footage, a brain scan, or a depiction of emotion measurement.

## Exclusions

No real researcher likenesses, clinical imagery, medical claims, brain heat maps, addiction claims, logos, subtitles rendered into source visuals, or implication that a single neural signal equals pleasure, happiness or moral agency.
