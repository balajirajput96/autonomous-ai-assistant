## Asset brief

Create six coherent 9:16 editorial illustrations for an educational Hindi social reel. Use a calm midnight-blue, muted-teal and warm-amber palette; realistic paper, desk and calendar motifs; no text, no logos, no celebrity likenesses, no brain scan imagery, no medical imagery, and ample centre-safe area for captions. Keep the visual language consistent across all six scenes.
